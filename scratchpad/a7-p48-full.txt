1 A margin of error (MOE) is a measure of an estimate’s variability. The larger the MOE in relation to the size of the estimate, the less reliable the estimate. This number,
when added to and subtracted from the estimate, forms the 90 percent confidence interval. MOEs shown in this table are based on standard errors calculated using
replicate weights beginning with 2010. Before 2010, standard errors were calculated using the generalized variance function.
2 Implementation of 2020 Census-based population controls.
3 Estimates reflect the implementation of an updated processing system and should be used to make comparisons to 2018 and subsequent years.

remaining 30,000 addresses were eligible to receive the redesigned income questions. The source of these 2013 estimates is the portion of the CPS ASEC sample that
received the redesigned income questions, approximately 30,000 addresses.
